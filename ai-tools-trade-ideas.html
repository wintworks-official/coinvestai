What Is Trade Ideas?

Trade Ideas is a US-focused market-scanning and trading platform best known for Holly AI, its machine-learning engine that surfaces probabilistic intraday setups across US equities. First launched nearly two decades ago as a professional scanner, it has evolved into a full day-trading workstation with simulated trading, strategy backtesting, and direct broker execution.

Unlike general charting tools, Trade Ideas is purpose-built for active day traders: its value proposition is real-time signal generation, not portfolio management. Holly runs multiple machine-learning strategies overnight and presents the highest-probability setups each morning before the open.

Core Capabilities in Practice

Holly AI engine: real-time probabilistic setup validation across large- and mid-cap US equities, with entry, stop, and target levels expressed in plain language.

Strategy backtesting (OddsMaker): historical simulation of entry/exit rules across your own watchlists, so you can validate ideas before risking capital.

Simulated trading: a full trading simulator that lets newcomers practice against live market data — widely regarded as one of the best learning environments for day trading.

Broker integration: webhook-based order routing to connected brokers for automated or semi-automated execution.

Pricing (as of mid-2026)

Trade Ideas is published in two main tiers: a Standard plan at roughly $89/month (billed annually) without Holly, and a Premium plan including Holly AI at roughly $178/month on annual billing — higher on monthly billing. A free trial is offered periodically. These rates change; confirm on the official site before subscribing.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: deeply integrated AI signal engine; excellent simulator; strong scanning speed; mature product with an active day-trading community.

Limitations: US equities only — no crypto or forex; premium AI tier is one of the most expensive retail subscriptions in the category; the interface carries a learning curve for complete beginners.

Who Is It For?

Active US-equity day traders who want AI-assisted signal generation and are willing to pay for institutional-grade scanning. Casual investors and buy-and-hold users will get little value from it — TradingView or TrendSpider are better fits for research-first workflows.

Common Questions

Is Trade Ideas suitable for beginners?

Not as a first platform. Its value is AI-assisted day trading, and its interface assumes market fluency. Beginners who commit to day trading should start in its simulated-trading mode and paper trade for at least two to three months.

Does Holly AI guarantee profits?

No. Holly surfaces probabilistic setups with defined risk levels — vendor and community reports indicate mid-50% historical win rates, which still means roughly half of signals fail. Risk management remains the trader's job.

Can I trade crypto or forex on Trade Ideas?

No. It is US-equities only. For crypto or multi-asset coverage, look at TradingView or dedicated crypto platforms.

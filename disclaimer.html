Hypothetical or simulated performance results have certain inherent limitations. Unlike an actual performance record, simulated results do not represent actual trading. 4. Artificial Intelligence Model Limitations & Hallucinations

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs), semantic search engines, and quantitative reasoning algorithms (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and DeepSeek) are subject to distinct technical limitations, including model hallucination — generating plausible-sounding yet factually or mathematically inaccurate statements.

When reviewing or utilizing generative AI outputs for investment due diligence, you must acknowledge that:

  • AI models are static snapshots trained on historical data and may lack real-time macroeconomic context.
  • Linguistic sentiment scoring is a probabilistic estimation and does not represent direct corporate execution.
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines can occasionally fail to parse complex accounting footnotes or multi-dimensional tables accurately.

CoinvestAI urges all analysts to apply systematic skepticism and independently verify every AI-generated metric, SEC filing extraction, and algorithmic code line against audited regulatory filings and primary source code before deployment.

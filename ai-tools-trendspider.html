What Is TrendSpider?

TrendSpider is a charting and technical-analysis automation platform aimed at serious technical analysts. Its signature feature is automated pattern and trendline detection: instead of drawing levels manually, TrendSpider identifies trendlines, Fibonacci levels, support/resistance zones, and multi-timeframe patterns automatically, then monitors them for you in real time.

Core Capabilities in Practice

Automated technical analysis: auto trendlines, multi-timeframe analysis, pattern recognition, and Raindrop charts — a proprietary volume-distribution visualization.

Strategy backtesting: a visual, no-code backtest builder — define entry and exit rules by dragging and dropping, and the engine simulates results across historical data.

Smart alerts and webhooks: conditional alerts ("price AND daily RSI below 35") that can trigger webhook automations for execution through connected services.

Coverage: equities, futures, forex, and crypto markets, with extensive historical data depth.

Pricing (as of mid-2026)

TrendSpider publishes tiered subscriptions starting at roughly $52/month when billed annually (around $82 month-to-month), with higher tiers unlocking the AI Trading Model builder, more scans, and additional alerts. Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: best-in-class charting automation; genuine time savings for active technical analysts; no-code backtesting; broad market coverage.

Limitations: steeper learning curve than TradingView; automation outputs still require trader judgment; higher-tier AI features sit behind more expensive plans.

Who Is It For?

Technical analysts and systematic traders who spend significant time on manual charting and want to automate the mechanics without writing Pine Script. Beginners can grow into it, but TradingView offers a gentler entry point.

Common Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. TrendSpider's backtesting and alerts are visual and rule-based — a deliberate contrast to Pine Script on TradingView. Developers can still go further elsewhere, but TrendSpider targets no-code automation.

Which markets are covered?

US equities, futures, forex, and a solid set of crypto markets, with multi-timeframe data — broader coverage than equities-only scanners such as Trade Ideas.

Is the automated analysis reliable?

The automation is mathematically consistent — trendlines, levels, and patterns are detected without the subjectivity of manual drawing. Consistency is not the same as edge, though: you still validate ideas with backtesting and judgment.

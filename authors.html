How We Assign Bylines

CoinvestAI publishes under two kinds of byline, and we are explicit about which is which because it affects how much weight a reader should give a single name.

Named bylines identify an individual analyst who researched and drafted the piece, and who is accountable for its claims. Named bylines carry a listed coverage area, and analysts only write inside it.

Desk bylines identify work produced collaboratively \u2014 comparative platform testing, methodology documents, and research that requires more than one specialism. Desk pieces still have a named author and a named reviewer internally; the desk byline reflects shared accountability for the result rather than anonymity.

Every article, regardless of byline, is reviewed under our 7-factor methodology and produced according to our editorial guidelines, including the correction policy and the disclosure of any commercial relationship with a vendor named in the piece.

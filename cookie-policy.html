Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files placed onto your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit websites. They are widely deployed across the internet to ensure platforms function smoothly, improve page rendering performance, store reader preferences, and provide operational telemetry to website operators.

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

CoinvestAI classifies cookies into four distinct operational categories:

  • Strictly Necessary & Security Cookies: Essential for foundational security, load balancing, SSL encryption integrity, and page routing. The site cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: Aggregate anonymized metrics regarding reader journey paths, average reading duration, and interaction frequencies to help us optimize article clarity.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies: Placed by third-party advertising partners, primarily Google AdSense, to serve relevant advertisements aligned with user interests across internet properties.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Maintain your custom interface settings, such as keyword filtering states in our interactive AI Tools Directory.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, utilize cookies (such as the DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve contextual and interest-based advertisements based on prior visits to CoinvestAI or other internet destinations.

Google Advertising Cookie Compliance:
Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized and non-personalized ads to our visitors based on their visit history across digital publications.

You can manage, restrict, or opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting the following consumer preference tools:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • AboutAds.info Consumer Choice Portal
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)

4. Discussion & Comment Privacy (Cusdis Integration)

CoinvestAI uses Cusdis for article discussions and community comments. Unlike legacy commenting widgets:

  • Cusdis does not inject invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies into your browser.
  • It utilizes minimal, privacy-centric local storage strictly to remember your submitted nickname during an active browsing session.

5. How to Manage & Disable Cookies in Your Browser

You maintain full control over cookie storage. Most web browsers allow you to inspect, block, or delete cookies via their built-in privacy settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies / Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.
Notice on Functional Restrictions: Disabling strictly necessary or preference cookies may cause certain interactive features—such as instant directory searching or category filtering—to function with reduced responsiveness.

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may periodically update this Cookie Policy to reflect technical infrastructure changes, advertising network updates, or evolving regulatory mandates (such as the EU ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, and CCPA). Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated timestamp.

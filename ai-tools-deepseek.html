1. Open-Weights Architecture & Why It Matters for Finance

Most AI tools reviewed on CoinvestAI — ChatGPT, Claude, AlphaSense — are closed systems where the model weights are proprietary, computation happens on vendor servers, and you have no control over data routing. For institutional finance, this creates an uncomfortable reality: your proprietary signals, client portfolio data, and unpublished research all flow through third-party infrastructure.

DeepSeek breaks this constraint by releasing model weights under permissive licensing, while simultaneously delivering reasoning capabilities that match or exceed closed frontier models. Its technical moat for financial applications rests on four architectural innovations:

🧠

Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA)

Radically compresses KV-cache memory overhead during inference — allowing quantitative teams to process massive multi-year financial ledgers and large context documents using significantly fewer GPU resources than conventional transformer architectures.

🔬

DeepSeek-R1 Reinforcement Learning

Pure RL reasoning architecture that explores multi-step chain-of-thought pathways, cross-verifying intermediate mathematical steps before outputting final quantitative derivations — critical for financial modeling where precision cascades through multiple calculation layers.

⚡

DeepSeekMoE Architecture

Sparse Mixture-of-Experts routing activating only necessary expert parameters per token — cutting inference compute costs by 60-80% without degrading analytical output quality, enabling cost-competitive on-premise deployment at hedge fund scale.

🔓

Distilled Open Models

Distilled variants (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B, Qwen-32B) enable local execution on high-end consumer hardware — Apple Silicon workstations or dual RTX 4090 rigs — democratizing frontier reasoning for smaller quant teams without HPC budgets.

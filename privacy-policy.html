Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to CoinvestAI (accessible at https://coinvestai.com/). We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data transparency, user privacy, and integrity. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, store, and safeguard your personal information when you access our independent financial research publication, directory, and interactive articles.

By using CoinvestAI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree with any terms herein, please discontinue the use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver high-quality quantitative research, ensure platform security, and improve reader experience. Information is obtained through direct submissions and automated telemetry.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact & Editorial Inquiries: Your name, email address, and message contents submitted via our contact forms or author communications.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address when you voluntarily subscribe to our market research and AI fintech briefings.
  • Community Comments & Discussions: Public nickname and optional email address submitted when engaging in article discussion sections.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Server Log Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser client version, operating system, referring URL source, timestamp, and requested page paths.
  • Aggregated Analytical Metrics: Session duration, viewport resolution, scroll depth, and interaction frequencies used to optimize our interface.

3. Discussion & Comment System (Cusdis Privacy Integration)

CoinvestAI employs Cusdis, an open-source, privacy-first comment system designed specifically to respect reader autonomy. Unlike traditional comment widgets:

  • Cusdis does not track users across third-party websites or build cross-domain behavioral profiles.
  • Users do not need to connect external social media accounts or create third-party logins to participate.
  • Any optional email address provided during comment submission is utilized solely for comment moderation notifications and is never monetized or shared.

4. Cookies, Tracking & Google AdSense Compliance

CoinvestAI uses cookies and similar web technologies to analyze network traffic and serve advertisements. For full technical details and browser configuration options, visit our dedicated Cookie Policy.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosure:
Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on a user's prior visits to CoinvestAI or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its advertising network partners to serve personalized and non-personalized ads tailored to user browsing trends. You can manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • AboutAds.info Consumer Choice Portal
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)

5. Legal Frameworks: GDPR, CCPA & Global Privacy Rights

CoinvestAI upholds statutory privacy rights under the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and UK Data Protection Laws.

Your Statutory Rights Include:

  • Right to Access: Request full confirmation of what personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request the permanent deletion of your email address, contact history, or posted comments.
  • Right to Rectification: Request corrections to any outdated or inaccurate personal records.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to specific data processing activities.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: CoinvestAI does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or contact details to third-party data brokers.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, please submit an official request to our Data Protection team at privacy@coinvestai.com.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

CoinvestAI provides specialized financial technology, quantitative trading, and blockchain intelligence intended for adults and institutional readers. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has submitted personal data to our website, please contact us immediately for swift deletion.

7. Data Security & Third-Party Links

We deploy robust organizational and technical safeguards, including HTTPS protocol encryption, restricted database access, and continuous server monitoring. However, no digital transmission over the global Internet is 100% invulnerable.

Our publications frequently contain hyperlinks to external financial providers, developer repositories, and academic research papers. CoinvestAI holds no responsibility or liability for the content, security, or privacy policies of third-party platforms.

8. Policy Amendments

We periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect advancements in technology, legal requirements, or changes to our editorial workflows. Any revisions will be published on this page accompanied by an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.

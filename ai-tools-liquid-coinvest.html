What Is Liquid Co-Invest?

Liquid Co-Invest represents an emerging class of financial technology: conversational AI directly interfaced with a multi-asset execution platform. Rather than forcing quantitative researchers and retail traders to manually move between standalone market terminals, data aggregators, and trading screens, Co-Invest bridges a user's Liquid account with large language models such as Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s ChatGPT.

The core objective of Co-Invest is to allow market participants to interrogate live market structure using natural language, evaluate portfolio positions, query order-book depth, and seamlessly transition from qualitative research into a structured trade proposal.

Technically implemented through modern interoperability standards like the Model Context Protocol (MCP), Co-Invest acts as a secure middleware layer. It exposes real-time market data, telemetry, and execution routines to the AI client while strictly preserving manual confirmation boundaries for all live trading actions.

CoinvestAI Editorial Perspective: Co-Invest is not simply a standard AI chatbot responding with static knowledge. Its utility lies in its live connection to venue data and portfolio telemetry. However, users must treat conversational AI as a research accelerator rather than an autonomous financial oracle.

How Co-Invest Works

The operational workflow of Co-Invest reverses the conventional order of trading operations. Instead of manually inspecting charts, opening an order ticket, calculating position sizes, and inputting leverage settings, the process starts with a conversational research query.

When a user queries the connected AI assistant regarding market conditions—for example, evaluating funding rate spikes or open-interest divergence across perpetual futures—the AI calls specific endpoints to fetch real-time market telemetry. It synthesizes this information and returns a structured response.

If the user decides to act upon the analysis, they can ask the AI to formulate a trade. Crucially, Co-Invest does not execute trades silently. Liquid's architecture routes the proposed order through a distinct confirmation layer where parameters—such as asset pair, direction (long/short), order type (limit/market), size, leverage, stop-loss, and take-profit—must be explicitly verified and approved by the user.

Step-by-Step Workflow

  1. Connection & Authentication: Connect the supported AI environment (e.g., Claude Desktop or ChatGPT) to Liquid Co-Invest via secure credentials.
  2. Permission Approval: Authorize read/write scope parameters while maintaining account security.
  3. Natural Language Query: Prompt the assistant to analyze live market telemetry, technical setups, or portfolio risk metrics.
  4. Synthesis & Evaluation: Review the AI's data retrieval, market context, and scenario analysis.
  5. Trade Formulation: Request a structured order proposal tailored to specific risk tolerance levels.
  6. Explicit Confirmation: Inspect the order parameters generated on the confirmation screen.
  7. Execution & Monitoring: Confirm the submission to route the order to Liquid's execution engine or paper trading environment.

Key Features & Architecture

  • Conversational Telemetry Parsing: Interrogate order books, liquidation levels, and funding rates via plain language.
  • Model Context Protocol Integration: Standardized protocol connectivity ensuring low-latency data streaming between LLM clients and execution venues.
  • Portfolio Context Awareness: The assistant can contextualize trade recommendations against active margin utilization, unexecuted limit orders, and current asset exposure.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Execution: Enforced manual confirmation modal ensuring no order is submitted without explicit user verification.
  • Simulated Paper Trading: Test prompts, complex order structures, and quantitative hypotheses using virtual capital on live market feeds.
  • Multi-Asset Scope: Cross-market coverage spanning digital assets, equity indices, commodities, and foreign exchange.

AI Market Research & Analytics

A major limitation of generic AI models in financial analysis is data latency and lack of context. While a standard LLM can explain derivative mechanics, it lacks awareness of real-time market shifts. Co-Invest addresses this gap by injecting live platform telemetry directly into the model's context window.

According to Liquid’s documentation, the research interface provides access to real-time spot and derivative market telemetry. Key data points accessible to the AI include:

  • Funding Rates & Predicted Yields: Tracking capital costs across crypto perpetual contracts.
  • Open Interest (OI) Movements: Identifying institutional position building or leverage unwinding.
  • Liquidation Clusters: Locating high-density liquidation zones that could trigger cascade events.
  • Order-Book Imbalance: Evaluating bid/ask depth to estimate potential slippage on large orders.
Data Accuracy Warning: Access to rich data streams does not guarantee profitable outcomes. Market conditions remain volatile, and AI models may occasionally misinterpret rapid order-book anomalies during high-impact market news.

Trading & Order Confirmation Controls

Co-Invest bridges theoretical research and actionable trading. Supported execution parameters include market orders, limit orders, post-only orders, and conditional triggers such as take-profit (TP) and stop-loss (SL) brackets.

The safety architecture centers on the Explicit Confirmation Gateway. When an AI formulates a trade, it outputs an unexecuted trade intent object. The user must review this on a dedicated confirmation interface, ensuring full visibility over leverage settings, margin allocation, and estimated liquidation prices before the trade enters the venue order book.

Leverage & Derivatives Risk: Leveraged derivative instruments carry significant financial risk. A confirmation window prevents unauthorized order submission but cannot protect a trader from market volatility, slippage, or position liquidation if the underlying market moves against the position.

Paper Trading & Simulation Environment

Paper trading is an essential component for evaluating AI-driven financial tools. Co-Invest provides a full simulation mode that mirror live order-book liquidity and market pricing without risking real capital.

Primary Benefits of the Simulation Environment:

  • Prompt Refinement: Learn how specific phrasing and parameters alter the AI's trade formulation logic.
  • Workflow Familiarization: Practice navigating the confirmation window under fast-moving market conditions.
  • Risk Model Validation: Test automated stop-loss and position-sizing prompts across historical volatility scenarios.
  • Zero Capital Risk: Experiment safely before deploying live collateral.

Permissions & Security Infrastructure

Granting an AI client access to financial execution channels requires robust security protocols. Liquid implements scoped permission controls based on the principle of least privilege.

Key security guardrails established in Liquid Co-Invest include:

  • No Withdrawal Scope: The Co-Invest connection lacks API endpoints or authorization pathways to execute capital withdrawals or transfers.
  • Isolated Execution Gateways: All order placements require interactive cryptographic confirmation from the account holder.
  • Session Expiration & Revocation: Users can instantly terminate active AI connections from the Liquid account security panel.

Investment Research Use Cases

1. Rapid Multi-Market Screening

Traders can ask the assistant to scan across multiple asset categories to highlight instruments exhibiting unusual open-interest expansion combined with low funding rates, speeding up preliminary research.

2. Dynamic Risk & Position Sizing

By requesting position sizing based on fixed percentage equity risk (e.g., "Calculate a long position on BTC with a 1.5% maximum portfolio risk and a stop-loss at $88,000"), traders can avoid manual math errors during execution.

3. Conversational Earnings & News Synthesis

Users can prompt the system to cross-reference macro announcements or company earnings reports with live asset price reactions to identify momentum divergence.

Limitations & Operational Risks

  • Ecosystem Dependence: Co-Invest functions exclusively within the Liquid platform framework.
  • Model Hallucinations: Large language models can occasionally output flawed logic or misinterpret nuanced financial data.
  • Slippage & Volatility Risk: Rapid market moves can cause execution slippage between trade confirmation and venue filling.
  • Technical Latency: Interfacing through multi-party API layers (LLM to MCP to Venue) adds latency compared to direct algorithmic API trading.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Liquid Co-Invest an official product of CoinvestAI?

No. Liquid Co-Invest is a third-party financial technology product operated by Liquid. CoinvestAI is an independent publication reviewing the platform.

Can Liquid Co-Invest execute trades automatically without my knowledge?

No. The Co-Invest architecture enforces an explicit human-in-the-loop confirmation modal. Every trade proposal must be manually approved by the user.

Can Co-Invest withdraw funds from my Liquid account?

No. The authorization scopes granted to Co-Invest do not include withdrawal or capital transfer permissions.

How much does Liquid Co-Invest cost?

Liquid offers the Co-Invest connection feature for free. Standard venue trading fees and funding rates apply to executed orders, alongside any separate subscription fees for your AI client (e.g., Claude Pro).

Is paper trading supported on Liquid Co-Invest?

Yes. Users can run simulated paper trading using virtual funds against real-time live venue data feeds.

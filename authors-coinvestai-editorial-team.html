How a CoinvestAI Article Is Produced

Every published piece passes the same six stages. We describe the process because a byline without a workflow behind it is not evidence of anything.

  1. Scoping and conflict check. The topic is assigned to an analyst inside their declared coverage area. Any commercial relationship with a vendor that will be named is identified at this stage, not at publication.
  2. Primary-source research. Claims are traced to vendor documentation, regulatory text, filings, peer-reviewed literature, or our own testing. Secondary commentary is not treated as a source.
  3. Hands-on testing where applicable. Platform and tool reviews run a fixed task battery, executed multiple times to separate capability from run-to-run variance. Single-run results are never published as findings.
  4. Scoring under the published methodology. Ratings come from the weighted 7-factor framework. Weights are fixed before scoring and cannot be adjusted after results are known.
  5. Independent second review. A second analyst outside the drafting desk checks sourcing, arithmetic, and whether limitations are stated as prominently as strengths.
  6. Compliance and disclaimer pass. The Ethics & Compliance reviewer confirms that risk language, disclaimers, and regulatory characterisations are accurate, and that no claim implies guaranteed returns or loss prevention.

Corrections are published on the article itself with the date and substance of the change. We do not silently edit published figures. To report an error, a broken source, or an undisclosed conflict, use our contact page.

Our Editorial Commitment

Independent Research

We are beholden only to our readers. Ratings are based on data, not paid placements. No vendor can purchase coverage, a score, or a position, and no score has ever been negotiated.

Fact-Driven Insights

Every claim is verified against primary sources, ensuring accuracy in a fast-moving AI landscape. Where evidence is thin or contested, we say so explicitly rather than presenting a confident summary of it.

Human Oversight

While we use AI to analyse data, final analysis is always vetted by human experts who are named internally and accountable for the result. We disclose where AI tooling was used in production.

Transparency

We openly disclose our review methodology, scoring frameworks, weighting, affiliate relationships, and advertising arrangements — including the fact that contextual advertising supports this publication.

Limitations Stated Prominently

Every review includes what the tool cannot do and where it fails. A review that lists only strengths is marketing, and we treat the weakness section as the more important half.

No Performance Promises

We never publish claims of guaranteed profit, assured accuracy, risk-free returns, or loss prevention. Any vendor making such claims is identified as a red flag in our evaluation framework.
